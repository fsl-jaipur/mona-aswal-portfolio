## Visual System
- Palette: coral `#FF6B6B`, raspberry `#EE5A70`, plum `#C44569`, violet `#574B90`, supported by warm paper and dark ink neutrals.
- Typography: Abril Fatface for large expressive display text; Cabin for readable supporting copy.
- Tactile paper grain, registration marks, folds, clipped edges, tape-like labels, and restrained translucency.
- Sharp or lightly softened edges; no SaaS-style card grid, generic gradient blobs, or conventional centered portfolio sections.
- Adapt the broken-grid composition for mobile without overlapping or clipped text.

## Motion
- Gentle staggered motion in Mona’s name.
- Paper layers drift subtly and settle as sections enter view.
- Project images reveal like pulled print proofs.
- Hover motion stays restrained and respects reduced-motion settings.
