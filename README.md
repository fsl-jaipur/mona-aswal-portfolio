# Mona's Creative Canvas

Design a portfolio website for a graphic designer with 8 years of experience in FMCG and e-commerce branding. Don't want a standard portfolio website. Design something which will stand out from the crowd.
References: 
https://www.behance.net/gallery/242575075/Portfolio-Graphic-Design-Branding
What I like in this one: bold fonts, peppy colors

https://www.behance.net/gallery/252076827/GRAPHIC-DESIGN-PORTFOLIO-2026
What I like in this one: the animations, use of paper image as background, bold fonts.

Also look at this version I built, but this is a little boring: https://graficdesignportfolio.vercel.app/

What I am looking for on the home page: Mona's caricature(photo attached) with her name in bold & Peppy font with slight animation, her designation. one liner describing her career so far.
Her services (categories she deals in), the tools she works with, her projects and her career timeline.
You can find all her designs here: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1QVAhcQttHsF5KMsfxmzEWzdhEfO3X1Tx
